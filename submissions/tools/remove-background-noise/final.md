## pricing_note

The free tier is a 30-second cleaned preview, and the homepage says no account is needed for that, though the FAQ separately says signing up is what unlocks free credits and the full experience. Paid options are one-time minute packs valid for a year: 1 hour for $3.49, 5 hours for $6.99, 10 hours for $11.99. PRO runs $9.90 a month for unlimited cleaning, a priority queue and cancel-anytime terms, though no fair-use ceiling is published for that "unlimited" claim. The cheapest way to walk away with one finished download is still the $3.49 pack. Terms of service say refunds are handled "case-by-case," but the refund policy that sentence points to isn't actually published anywhere, and no company name or jurisdiction appears on the site either. costPerMonth here reflects the $9.90 PRO plan, the only recurring tier. All prices are in USD.
